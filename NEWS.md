# repdist 0.99.0

* First Bioconductor submission: protein embeddings from a frozen protein
  language model (`embed_proteins()`), abundance-weighted RBF-MMD sample
  distances (`sample_repdist()`), complete-linkage structural bins
  (`bin_proteins()`) and three quality-control plots.
* Worked metaproteome and simulation vignettes run on cached embeddings.
